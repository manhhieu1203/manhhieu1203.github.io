Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This policy applies to the game Knife Ops: Stealth Commando ("the game") by MH Fusion Interactive.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not run servers that receive data from the game, and we do not sell or share personal data. The game has no accounts of its own, no ads and no advertising ID. Some data is collected by the store and purchase services the game uses; they are listed below with what they collect.

Data on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your device. We cannot see this data. Deleting the game deletes the copy on that device.

iCloud (Apple)

On iPhone and iPad, your progress (level results, rank, found intel, story progress) and some settings (language, music, sound, hints, reduced motion) are also synced through your own iCloud account, so they come back after a reinstall or on another device with the same Apple ID. This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud; we cannot read it. You can turn it off in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game reports achievements and a total-time leaderboard score to Apple under your Game Center account. Game Center is optional and managed by Apple; Apple's privacy policy applies.

Google Play Games (Android)

On Android, the game uses Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Play Games, the game sends achievements, a total-time leaderboard score and one cloud save (level results, rank, found intel, story progress and some settings) to Google under your Play Games profile, so your progress comes back after a reinstall or on another device. Google also receives your gamer profile and SDK diagnostics. Play Games is optional and managed by Google. You can delete this data in your Play Games profile or Google Account. Google's privacy policy applies.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple on iOS and by Google Play on Android. We do not receive your payment details. The store's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

The game handles purchases with Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity Technologies). To process and check purchases, this library collects your purchase history, device and purchase identifiers, approximate location, and crash and diagnostic data. On Android, the email address of your store account may be processed briefly and is not stored by us. This data is used for the purchase function and for analytics of the purchase service. It is not used for ads. Unity's privacy policy applies.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. The services above collect only the data described on this page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date.
