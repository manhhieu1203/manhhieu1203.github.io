Frequently asked questions

I bought the full mission, but it is locked on my new device.
Select a level of act 2 to open the unlock screen, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Is my progress saved?
Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes the progress.

Which languages are there?
The game has 20 languages. Change the language in Settings.
