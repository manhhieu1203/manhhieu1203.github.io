Frequently asked questions

I bought all levels, but they are locked on my new device.
Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

What does the purchase include?
Levels 1-15 are free. One purchase opens levels 16-45. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

Is my progress saved?
Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes the progress.

I am stuck on a level.
Tap Undo or Restart. Every level has a solution.
